# Organon

Here is a collection of small things to support my lessons on developing
electronic musical instruments - mainly they are targeted to teach and
demonstrate natural scale.

More elaborate description is yet to be added.

For now you can play with creating musical scale with fractions.

Visit: https://rodiongork.github.io/organon/ and 

For consistency with note names change "base frequency" to 264 and then
add a few notes:

- "C" with multiplier 1/1
- "G" with multiplier 3/2
- "E" with multiplier 5/4

at this point you can click the buttons which appear and you'll hear that these
three fractions create "major chord" i.e. triad with distinctive "joyful" sound.

If you would like to hear the "minor chord" (sad triad), add "Eb" with multiplier 6/5 and
play C-Eb-G for example. You'll learn that minor and major chord differ by just one note
(so much the distance between joy and sorrow).

Proceed by adding remaining "base" notes of the scale, e.g.

- "F" with multiplier 4/3
- "D" with multiplier 9/8
- "A" with multiplier 5/3
- "B" with multiplier 15/8 (I believe it is called "H" in German sources)

You may learn the idea behind these fractions looking for "natural scale" or "5-limit tuning"
or "Ptolemy's intense diatonic scale" in the wikipedia (I'm not adding direct links as these
pages are sometimes reworked by enthusiasts trying to make them more clear - but retretfully
it only adds confusion).

Additional files (sectors.html, ds.html, circles.html, rhombi.html) are for generating image
of the "sound disc" which is used in opto-mechanical demonstration of the principle. Hopefully
video will be added soon to show how it works.
